# Village After Dark

Village After Dark is a social deduction game of deception, suspicion, and survival. Every player is dealt a hidden role, and the village must survive the night, expose the wicked, and decide who deserves to be voted out by day.

## The Objective

Each role belongs to a team or alignment with its own victory condition.

- Townsfolk win by removing evil influence.
- The Thieves win by eliminating their rivals.
- Independent Evil roles pursue their own dangerous goals.

## The Game Flow

### Night
Certain roles act in secret. They may protect allies, investigate, block others, or strike at their targets.

### Day
The surviving players gather, discuss, accuse, and judge one another.

### Voting
The village votes on who should be sent away. A single wrong accusation can change the course of the whole game.

### Survival
Players can be eliminated by attacks, execution, or special effects. The game continues until one side wins.

---

## Alignments

### Townsfolk
The good side. Their goal is to protect the village and expose evil.

### Thieves
A hidden evil team that works together and knows one another.

### Independent Evil
Ruthless roles that act alone and follow their own win conditions.

  <img src="GameImages/Roles/Good/Female/2.Female_Healer.png" alt="Healer female" width="120" />

### Guard — Townsfolk Support
<div>
  <img src="GameImages/Roles/Good/Male/11.Male_Guard.png" alt="Guard male" width="120" />
  <img src="GameImages/Roles/Good/Female/11.Female_Guard.png" alt="Guard female" width="120" />
</div>

At night, the Guard may choose another player or pass. If a killer attacks the guarded player, the player survives while the Guard and killer die. The killer receives the Guard action image at the start of the day. Guard is available as a role but is not in the preset scenarios.

### Tavern Keeper — Townsfolk Blocking
<div>
  <img src="GameImages/Roles/Good/Male/3.Male_TavernKeeper.png" alt="Tavern Keeper male" width="120" />
  <img src="GameImages/Roles/Good/Female/3.Female_TavernKeeper.png" alt="Tavern Keeper female" width="120" />
</div>

The Tavern Keeper blocks another player’s ability for the night, preventing them from acting.

### Jailor — Townsfolk Blocking
<div>
  <img src="GameImages/Roles/Good/Male/12.Male_Jailor.png" alt="Jailor male" width="120" />
  <img src="GameImages/Roles/Good/Female/12.Female_Jailor.png" alt="Jailor female" width="120" />
</div>

The Jailor may jail one player or pass. The jailed player cannot act, and night actions aimed at them have no effect. Visitors are not told that their actions were stopped. The jailed player receives the Jailor action image privately.

### Militia — Townsfolk Investigate
<div>
  <img src="GameImages/Roles/Good/Male/4.Male_Militia.png" alt="Militia male" width="120" />
  <img src="GameImages/Roles/Good/Female/4.Female_Militia.png" alt="Militia female" width="120" />
</div>

The Militia investigates another player and learns their alignment, helping the village determine who is good or evil.

### Judge — Townsfolk Investigate
<div>
  <img src="GameImages/Roles/Good/Male/10.Male_Judge.png" alt="Judge male" width="120" />
  <img src="GameImages/Roles/Good/Female/10.Female_Judge.png" alt="Judge female" width="120" />
</div>

The Judge chooses two players at night and learns whether they are teammates or rivals, or may pass without choosing anyone. Choosing players counts as visiting both for night visit effects, including the Werewolf and Trapper. Players on the same Townsfolk or Thieves team are teammates; Independent Evil players always count as rivals, even when both are Independent Evil. Judge is available as a role but is not in the preset scenarios.

## Thieves Team

### Master Thief — Thieves Killing
<div>
  <img src="GameImages/Roles/Evil/Male/1.Male_MasterThief.png" alt="Master Thief male" width="120" />
  <img src="GameImages/Roles/Evil/Female/1.Female_MasterThief.png" alt="Master Thief female" width="120" />
</div>

The Master Thief leads the team and chooses the target. They work alongside the Thief and can appear as good to the Militia.

### Thief — Thieves Killing
<div>
  <img src="GameImages/Roles/Evil/Male/2.Male_Thief.png" alt="Thief male" width="120" />
  <img src="GameImages/Roles/Evil/Female/2.Female_Thief.png" alt="Thief female" width="120" />
</div>

The Thief performs the actual attack and works together with the Master Thief to eliminate the village.

### Thief Shaman — Thieves Support
<div>
  <img src="GameImages/Roles/Evil/Male/3.Male_Shaman.png" alt="Thief Shaman" width="120" />
  <img src="GameImages/Roles/Evil/Female/3.Female_Shaman.png" alt="Thief Shaman" width="120" />
</div>

The Thief Shaman knows the other Thieves, and they know the Shaman. While either the Master Thief or Thief is alive, the Shaman may silence one player for the day; that player cannot send chat messages and privately receives the silence image. The Shaman also sees the Thieves' selected kill target. If both other Thieves are dead, the Shaman can no longer silence and instead selects the team's kill target; the victim privately receives the Shaman kill image.

### Thief Seer — Thieves Support
<div>
  <img src="GameImages/Roles/Evil/Male/4.Male_Seer.png" alt="Thief Seer" width="120" />
  <img src="GameImages/Roles/Evil/Female/4.Female_Seer.png" alt="Thief Seer" width="120" />
</div>

At night, the Thief Seer may select a player to learn their exact role or pass. The whole living Thieves team privately receives the Seer vision image with the player's name and role. If the Master Thief, Thief, and Thief Shaman are all dead, the Seer instead selects the Thieves' kill target; the victim privately receives the Seer kill image.

## Final Notes

Village After Dark is a game of reading people, protecting allies, and surviving the chaos of night and day. Every role matters, every action has consequences, and the village can change in a single vote.
